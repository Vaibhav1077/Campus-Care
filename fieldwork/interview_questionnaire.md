# CampusCare – Interview Questionnaire
## For Hostel Warden / Maintenance Staff

**Purpose:** To understand the current complaint handling workflow and identify pain points that CampusCare is designed to address.

**Instructions for interviewer:** Read each question aloud. Record answers in `interview_notes.md`. Do not prompt or suggest answers.

### Questions

**Q1 – Current complaint intake**
How do students currently report a maintenance complaint to you? Do they come in person, call, send a message, or use any other method?

---

**Q2 – Recording complaints**
When a student reports a complaint, how do you record it? Do you use a register, a spreadsheet, a WhatsApp group, or something else?

---

**Q3 – Prioritising urgent issues**
How do you decide which complaints to attend to first? Is there a fixed rule, or does it depend on the situation?

---

**Q4 – Assigning work to staff**
How do you assign a complaint to a maintenance worker? Do you call them directly, write it in a book, or use any other method?

---

**Q5 – Common complaint types**
What are the most frequent types of maintenance problems you deal with — for example, electrical, plumbing, furniture, or something else?

---

**Q6 – Reasons for delay**
What are the main reasons a complaint sometimes takes longer than expected to resolve?

---

**Q7 – Recurring problems**
Are there rooms or areas in the hostel that report the same type of complaint repeatedly? Can you give an example?

---

**Q8 – Updating students**
How does a student find out that their complaint has been attended to or resolved? Do you inform them directly, or do they have to follow up?

---

**Q9 – Verifying resolution**
After a repair is done, how do you confirm it has been completed properly? Does the student or warden check the work?

---

**Q10 – Improvements needed**
If you could change one thing about how complaints are handled today, what would it be?

---

*End of questionnaire.*
